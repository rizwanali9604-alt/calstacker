Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Overview

CalStacker.com ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and financial calculators.

Calculator Data

All financial calculations are performed locally in your web browser. We do not require account creation, and we do not store the numbers you enter into our calculators on our servers.

Automatically Collected Information

Our hosting provider and advertising partners may receive standard request data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URL. Calculator inputs are not sent to our servers.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-RR873MXQNX) loads only after you click “Accept analytics” on the cookie banner. If you choose “Ads only”, we do not load Analytics from this site. Analytics may then set cookies and record page views and calculator-usage events. You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies & Advertising

We include the Google AdSense script (publisher ID ca-pub-8332278519903196) so advertisements can display and so Google can review the site. AdSense may set cookies even if you decline analytics. The banner does not claim that ads wait for a click. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or control cookies in your browser. See our Terms of Service.

Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites (brokers, tax filing services, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

Children's Privacy

CalStacker.com is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
