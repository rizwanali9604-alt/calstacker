About CalStacker

Built by Rizwan Ali for Indian salaried employees and investors

CalStacker is a free financial education platform for salaried employees and investors in India. We publish accurate calculators and plain-language guides so you can understand your salary, plan taxes, compare loans, and project investments — without signing up or sharing personal data.

Who runs CalStacker

Rizwan Ali builds and maintains CalStacker. He focuses on Indian personal finance tools — CTC-to-in-hand breakdowns, tax-regime comparisons, EMI planning, and SIP projections — using publicly available tax rules, EPFO notifications, and standard banking formulas.

CalStacker is an independent publisher. We are not a bank, stock broker, mutual fund distributor, or SEBI-registered investment adviser. Our goal is to make everyday money decisions clearer through free tools and guides.

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are never sent to our servers.

What we publish

  • 20 calculators — EMI, SIP, salary, income tax, HRA, EPF, PPF, FD, GST, retirement, and more
  • Financial guides — Long-form articles on salary structure, tax saving, SIP investing, home loans, and EPF
  • FAQs on every tool — How formulas work, what assumptions we use, and when to verify with a professional

Editorial standards

Content is researched from publicly available Indian tax rules, EPFO notifications, and standard financial formulas. Guides and calculator defaults are updated when major rule changes occur (for example new tax-regime slabs or EPF interest rates). Your employer or bank may use different structures — always verify figures that affect a real decision.

Guides are written by Rizwan Ali. We cite specific rupee amounts, state-level rules where relevant, and worked Indian examples. We welcome corrections at contact@calstacker.com.

Important disclaimer

CalStacker provides estimates for education and planning only. We do not provide personalised financial, tax, or legal advice. Always confirm important decisions with a qualified Chartered Accountant, tax professional, or SEBI-registered adviser. Read our full Disclaimer and Terms of Service.
