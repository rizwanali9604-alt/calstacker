About CalStacker

Built by Rizwan Ali for Indian salaried employees and investors

CalStacker is a free financial education site for salaried employees and investors in India. We publish calculators and plain-language guides so you can estimate EMI, SIP, in-hand salary, and income tax in the browser — without creating an account or sending us your numbers.

Who runs CalStacker

CalStacker is an independent publisher. We are not a bank, stock broker, mutual fund distributor, or SEBI-registered investment adviser. Our goal is to make everyday money decisions clearer through free tools and guides.

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are never sent to our servers.

What we publish

  • 20 calculators — EMI, SIP, salary, income tax, HRA, EPF, PPF, FD, GST, retirement, and more
  • Financial guides — Long-form articles on salary structure, tax saving, SIP investing, home loans, and EPF
  • Assumptions on every tool — How formulas work, which FY rules we used, and when to verify with a professional

Editorial standards

Content is researched from publicly available Indian tax rules, EPFO notifications, and standard financial formulas. Tax and salary tools carry a “last verified” date (currently 17 August 2026 for FY 2026-27 slabs). We update that date when we re-check a Budget or a rate circular. Your employer or bank may use different structures — always verify figures that affect a real decision.

Read the full editorial policy for sources, what we will not claim, and how to request a correction. Email contact@calstacker.com.

Important disclaimer

CalStacker provides estimates for education and planning only. We do not provide personalised financial, tax, or legal advice. Always confirm important decisions with a qualified Chartered Accountant, tax professional, or SEBI-registered adviser. Read our full Disclaimer and Terms of Service.
