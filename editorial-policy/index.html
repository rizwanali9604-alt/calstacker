Editorial policy

Last updated 17 August 2026

CalStacker publishes free calculators and guides about Indian personal finance. This page explains how we work, what we will and will not claim, and how to request a correction.

Who writes this

Pages are written and maintained by Rizwan Ali. CalStacker is an independent publisher. We are not a Chartered Accountant firm, a SEBI-registered investment adviser, a bank, or a mutual-fund distributor. Where a page names an author, that is the person accountable for the text and the formula on that page.

What our numbers are

Calculator outputs are estimates from standard formulas and publicly stated statutory rates. They are not an assessment order, a payslip, a bank quotation, or tax advice. Employers, banks, and AMCs use their own rounding, fee, and salary-structure rules. If a figure will change a real decision, confirm it with your CA, employer, or lender.

Sources we use

  • Income-tax slabs, rebate, surcharge, and cess — Finance Act / Income-tax Act, 2025 as applicable for FY 2026-27, cross-checked against incometax.gov.in
  • EPF contribution rules — epfindia.gov.in
  • HRA exemption — Section 10(13A) and Rule 2A (least of three conditions)
  • Loan EMI — reducing-balance formula used by Indian retail lenders
  • SIP projections — future value of an annuity due (instalment treated as start-of-month). Expected return is an input, not a forecast.

Tax and salary pages show a “last verified” date next to the rules they use. We update that date when we re-read the Budget or a rate circular — not automatically every day.

What we will not do

  • Promise that a result is “accurate” for your specific CTC, city, or return.
  • Invent traffic claims (“millions of users”) or professional credentials we do not have.
  • Publish placeholder affiliate buttons or unfinished “coming soon” money pages.
  • Give personalised investment or tax advice by email.

Corrections

If you find a wrong slab, a stale EPF rate, or a formula bug, email contact@calstacker.com with the page URL, the input you used, the number you got, and the number you expected. We aim to reply within 3–5 business days. Material formula fixes are noted on the relevant page’s verified date.

Advertising

The site may show Google AdSense ads after approval. Ads are not editorial recommendations. Advertising does not change calculator math. See Privacy and Disclaimer.
