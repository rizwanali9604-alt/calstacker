GST on an Indian invoice, both directions

Most consumer prices you see in an app are GST-inclusive. A shop bill for a laptop at ₹70,800 with 18% GST is not “₹70,800 plus tax”. Reverse the tax: base = 70,800 / 1.18 = ₹60,000; GST = ₹10,800. Exclusive mode does the opposite: ₹60,000 × 18% = ₹10,800; total ₹70,800.

This page splits intra-state tax as CGST + SGST (half each) and inter-state as IGST (full rate). It does not file GSTR-1 for you, does not decide your HSN code, and does not apply compensation cess (coal, cars, tobacco). If your item is cess-attracting, the total here will be low.

Rate is not a guess

Use the slab printed on the tax invoice or in the GST rate schedule for that HSN/SAC — 0, 5, 12, 18, or 28 are the common goods slabs, with 3% for some jewellery. Services are often 18%. Composition dealers do not charge GST the same way; do not use this calculator to “add 18%” on a composition invoice.

Turnover thresholds for mandatory registration (₹40 lakh goods / ₹20 lakh services, lower in some special-category states) change; confirm on the GST portal before you register. Guide: GST guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common GST rates are 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. Some items are nil-rated or exempt. Essential goods often attract 5%, while luxury items may be at 28%.
GST exclusive means the entered amount is before tax — GST is added on top. GST inclusive means the amount already includes tax — we back-calculate the base and GST portion.
Base = Total ÷ (1 + rate/100). For 18% GST on ₹11,800 inclusive: Base = 11,800 ÷ 1.18 = ₹10,000 and GST = ₹1,800.
For intra-state sales, GST splits equally into CGST and SGST (e.g. 9% + 9% = 18%). For inter-state, IGST applies at the full rate. This calculator shows combined GST.
Service suppliers often must register above ₹20 lakh turnover (₹10 lakh in some special-category states). Goods suppliers often have a higher threshold. Confirm the live limit on the GST portal before you charge tax — this page does not decide registration.
Many services and goods currently sit at 18%, but the legal rate is the HSN/SAC on the tax invoice — not a guess from this FAQ. Milk and many fresh foods are nil or exempt; cess items are outside this tool.
