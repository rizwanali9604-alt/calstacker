How we turn CTC into an in-hand estimate

A ₹12 lakh CTC is not ₹1 lakh a month in your account. This tool peels CTC in a fixed order:

  1. Gross pay = CTC − employer PF − (optional) gratuity accrual at 4.81% of basic. Employer PF is modelled as matching employee PF on PF wages (12% of min(basic, ₹15,000) per month). Many private offers also bury insurance and NPS in CTC; those are not modelled, so we can still overstate gross.
  2. Employee PF leaves the payslip for your EPF account (max ₹1,800/month on the wage cap).
  3. Professional tax uses the state dropdown. Karnataka is ₹200/month above the threshold; Maharashtra is ₹200 most months and ₹300 in February; Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Punjab and several others are ₹0. Pick “No PT” if your state is not listed with a rate.
  4. TDS uses FY 2026-27 slabs. Under the new regime, employee PF and professional tax do not reduce taxable income — only the ₹75,000 standard deduction does. Under the old regime they do, plus HRA if you enter rent.

That last point is the usual spreadsheet mistake: copying old-regime deductions into a new-regime in-hand sheet. If you compare two offers, run both in the same regime and the same state.

Karnataka vs Delhi, ₹12 LPA, new regime, 40% basic, PF and gratuity on

Gross is CTC minus employer PF (₹21,600) minus gratuity (~₹23,088) ≈ ₹11.55 lakh. Taxable after ₹75,000 standard deduction is under ₹12 lakh, so income-tax is typically nil after 87A. In-hand then differs mainly by professional tax: ~₹200/month in Karnataka, ₹0 in Delhi. Insurance, joining bonus, and ESOP are still missing — treat the result as a floor, not a promise. Frequently Asked Questions

CTC (Cost to Company) is the total annual package including employer contributions. In-hand (take-home) is what you receive after employee PF, professional tax, income tax (TDS), and other deductions.
Employee EPF is 12% of basic salary per month, capped at 12% of ₹15,000 (i.e. max ₹1,800/month) when basic exceeds ₹15,000. Employer also contributes a matching amount, included in CTC but not deducted from your in-hand twice.
Professional tax is a state levy. Use the state dropdown: Karnataka ₹200/month (typical salaried slab), Maharashtra ₹200 with ₹300 in February (₹2,500/year), several southern and western states similar, and ₹0 in Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Punjab, Rajasthan, Bihar and others. Amounts are simplified; your state’s slab notification can differ.
Metro vs non-metro changes HRA exemption (50% vs 40% of basic) when you enter rent under the old regime. It does not change new-regime TDS. Professional tax is chosen separately via the employment-state dropdown.
This calculator spreads annual estimated tax evenly across 12 months for projection. Employers may adjust TDS mid-year based on actual investments declared or bonus payouts.
